What is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a type of barcode that stores information. When you scan it with your phone camera, it instantly opens a website, shares text, or connects to WiFi.

Where Can I Use These QR Codes?

  • On business cards to share your website or contact info.
  • On restaurant menus to show the digital menu.
  • On posters or flyers to link to your social media pages.
  • At events to share WiFi passwords instantly.
