How to Use the Word Counter

Why Use a Word Counter?

Many platforms have strict character limits. Twitter/X allows 280 characters, meta descriptions should be under 160 characters, and academic essays often have strict word counts. This tool helps you stay within those limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text saved? No. Everything happens in your browser. Your text is never uploaded or stored.

Does it count spaces? Yes, the "Characters" count includes spaces. "No Spaces" shows the count without them.
