How to Compress an Image

  1. Tap the box above and select a JPG, PNG, or WebP image from your phone or computer.
  2. Choose your output format. JPEG gives the smallest file size. WebP is modern and efficient. PNG keeps transparency.
  3. Adjust the quality slider. 80% is a good balance. Lower it for smaller files.
  4. Tap Compress Image.
  5. Compare the before-and-after sizes, then tap Download.

Why Compress Images?

Large images slow down websites, fill up your phone storage, and take forever to send on WhatsApp or email. Compressing them keeps the visual quality while dramatically reducing the file size.

Is This Tool Free?

Yes, completely free with no signup and no limits. Your images never leave your device — all compression happens inside your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I lose image quality? At 80% quality, most people cannot tell the difference, but the file can be 50–80% smaller.

What format is best? JPEG for photos, WebP for web use, PNG only if you need a transparent background.

Does it work on iPhone and Android? Yes. The tool works on any modern phone browser.
